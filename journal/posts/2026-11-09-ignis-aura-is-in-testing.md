Ignis Aura is a fantasy tabletop adventure made to be held, with a companion app that runs the game. Beta testers are playing early builds on iPhone, Android and the web right now, and telling us what to change.

## Take the tour

We recorded a game straight from the app: gathering round, the Keeper of the Flame, a turn with a dice roll, a rune lock, a riddle and nightfall. [Watch it on the Ignis Aura page](/ignis/#tour-title).

## What this beta is

Right now Ignis Aura is an early, fully digital proof of concept, and we're building the groundwork and the flow of play. The physical pieces are still being designed, so for this beta **the whole experience is playable with just the app**. In the end, it's meant to be played with its physical pieces, and the app helps run it.

## How it plays

- **Keeper of the Flame.** One of you holds the phone, reads aloud and keeps the game moving. Sharing one phone? The app tells you when to pass it.
- **A turn.** Face what waits on your square, choose what to do, roll the dice, then move on. Roll your own dice, or let the app roll them.
- **Rune locks and riddles.** Turn the dials and read the clues the lock gives back. Some doors only open to the right answer.
- **Day and night.** The world turns from day to night every two rounds.
- **No signal needed.** Play solo, pass one phone around, or link everyone's phones, with no internet at all.

## How to join

[Join the beta on the Ignis Aura page](/ignis/#beta): sign up with your email and where you'd play, then confirm it from your inbox. We look at each sign-up ourselves, and email you when you're in, with a link for each place you picked:

- **iPhone and iPad:** through TestFlight, Apple's app for test versions.
- **Android:** through Google Play. Sign up with your Google account's email (usually Gmail), since Google Play only lets that one into the test.
- **Web:** in your browser.

Testers agree to the [Beta Tester Terms](/beta/terms/). You can also message us on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/creatioaura) or email [contact@creatio-aura.com](mailto:contact@creatio-aura.com).

## What's next

What's on the Ignis Aura page is only a glimpse of what the game already supports, and there's still a lot of writing, balancing and playtesting to do. We're working through testers' notes as they come in.
