Ignis Aura hasn't been announced yet. Everything about it that you see as a tester is confidential: the nature and type of game it is, its rules and how a game flows, its story, encounters and content, its design, art and music, what makes it fun, the builds themselves, and anything we tell you about it. Feedback is the point of the beta, and we're grateful for it. Anything you send us about Ignis Aura (ideas, suggestions, bug reports) we may use to improve the game, freely and without owing you anything for it.
