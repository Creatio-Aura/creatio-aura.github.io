Effugium Aura is an audio player for your own sounds. Friends and family are testing early builds on iPhone and Android right now, and telling us what to change before it goes to the App Store and Google Play.

## Take the tour

The app opens with a short tour, six steps from bringing in your own sounds to helping them loop more seamlessly. We recorded it straight from the app: [watch it on the Effugium Aura page](/effugium/#tour-title).

## What's in this test

- **Your own sounds.** Record them in the app, or bring them in from your files: the fan you're used to, the rain on your porch, a creek from a walk.
- **Sounds around you.** Put each one in front, behind, to either side, above or below.
- **Gravity.** Let your sounds drift around you on slow orbits, with the odd shooting star passing by.
- **The Kicker.** A sound, a vibration, or both, on a timer you set.
- **A sleep timer that fades.** The mix fades out when the timer ends, and it keeps playing with the screen off.

On iPhone it also follows your head with AirPods, and the Kicker can vibrate your Apple Watch.

## How to join

[Join the beta on the Effugium Aura page](/effugium/#beta): sign up with your email and whether you'd use it on an iPhone or an Android phone, then confirm it from your inbox. We'll email you as soon as there's a build for you. You can also message us on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/creatioaura) or email [contact@creatio-aura.com](mailto:contact@creatio-aura.com).

## What's next

We're working through testers' notes now. When it's ready, Effugium Aura will be in the App Store and on Google Play.
