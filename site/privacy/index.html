Privacy

This covers this website, signing up for our betas, feedback sent from our apps, and our waiting lists for projects in the works. Each app has its own privacy notes too: see Effugium Aura's. In short: we keep only what you type into our forms, use it only for what you signed up for, never sell or share it, and you can remove it any time.

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

Who we are

Creatio Aura Ltd., an independent studio. Contact us at privacy@creatio-aura.com.

What we collect, and why

When you sign up for one of our betas (Ignis Aura's or Effugium Aura's), we keep:

  • your email address, to email you about that beta;
  • where you'd play or use it (iPhone, Android, web), to send you the right build;
  • that you agreed to be emailed (and, for Ignis Aura, to the Beta Tester Terms), the words you agreed to, and when;
  • whether we've let you in yet, and when;
  • how you found the sign-up, as a short label: the page or button that brought you (for example, a post in our Journal), or the site you came from (for example, Google), or "direct". It's never the full web address, and we use it only to count which pages bring people to our betas.

Testers who signed up before 7 November 2026 also gave a name, and could write us notes; we keep those as well, until they leave.

That's all. We don't store your IP address. To stop abuse, we briefly count sign-up attempts per connection using an anonymous, scrambled fingerprint of it that can't be turned back into the address; those counts are deleted within two days.

We use your details only to run the beta: confirming your sign-up, and emailing you builds and news about it. We don't sell them, share them, or use them for advertising.

Confirming, and leaving

After you sign up, we email you a link to confirm. Once you confirm, we look at your sign-up, and when we let you in, we email you to say so, with how to get the beta. If you don't confirm, your details are deleted within 7 days, and if we don't let you in, they're deleted straight away. Every email we send has a link to remove yourself, which deletes your details straight away. You can also email us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it.

Waiting lists

On a project's page you can ask to hear when it's out. For that we keep your email, which project, and that you agreed to be emailed (the words you agreed to, and when). No name, and no IP address. We email you a link to confirm; if you don't confirm, your email is deleted within 7 days. Once you confirm, we email you to say you're on the list, and after that we'll only email you about that project's release. Every email has a link to leave, which deletes your email straight away.

Feedback from the apps

From the Effugium Aura app, Send feedback is anonymous: its privacy notes say what it sends. During the beta you can also send us feedback from inside the Ignis Aura app. We keep what you write, the email address you give if you'd like a reply (it's optional), and what the app sends with it so we can find a problem: the app's version, the kind of device, the screen you were on, and how far into the game you were. No name unless you type one, and no IP address. It comes to our inbox, and we use it only to improve the game and to answer you. If the app can't reach the internet, it keeps your note on your device and sends it when it can.

Where it's kept, and who helps

  • Cloudflare runs our sign-up service and stores the beta list, the waiting lists, feedback from the apps and Effugium Aura's anonymous counts (only from people who choose to share them) in private databases that only Creatio Aura can access. Cloudflare's Turnstile check, on the sign-up form, tells people from bots without puzzles or tracking cookies.
  • IONOS runs our @creatio-aura.com email addresses: it sends our emails, from support@creatio-aura.com, and passes what you send us on to our inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) holds our inbox. This site's fonts are served from this site itself, so loading a page sends nothing to Google.
  • GitHub hosts this website.

These providers may process data in the United States and elsewhere, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Beta sign-ups: until you remove yourself, or the beta ends, whichever comes first. Waiting lists: until you remove yourself, or we've emailed you that the project is out, whichever comes first. Unconfirmed sign-ups of either kind: 7 days. Feedback: two years after it's sent at most, or sooner: Ignis Aura's when its beta ends, or when you ask us to delete it. Effugium Aura's anonymous counts: two years.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. The only thing we note about how people find us is the short label kept with a beta sign-up, described above.

Children

Our betas and waiting lists are for people 18 and over. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we'll delete it.

The apps

This page is about the website, beta sign-ups, waiting lists and feedback sent from the apps. Effugium Aura's privacy notes cover what that app keeps and sends. Ignis Aura's privacy notes cover that app.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above, and email people on the beta list.
