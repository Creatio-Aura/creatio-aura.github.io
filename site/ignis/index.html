Ignis Aura

The companion app for a fantasy tabletop game, built to be played around a campfire. The board, dice and cards stay on the table; the app tells the story.

Beta sign-ups openiPhone · Android · Web

What's in it

Adventures and encounters

Pick a tile and a place; the app draws the encounter, tells the story and settles the outcome.

The digital board

Every hero's square across four tiles, travelled through their gates. Forgot the board? Play on the phone.

Your own phone, or one passed round

Each friend on their own phone, online or by Bluetooth, or one phone passed around the fire.

Dice, coins and locks

Roll real dice or the app's 3D ones; pick tilt-maze locks, crack rune locks, race quicktime flares.

The Chest of Fate

Great deeds earn a draw: break the seal, enter the runes from a real Fate card, claim the reward.

Heroes that grow

Six classes, eight stats, gear, gold, renown and levels: feed embers into your stats as you rise.
