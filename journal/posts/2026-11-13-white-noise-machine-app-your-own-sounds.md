A white noise machine plays one steady sound all night. Most come with a handful of built-in sounds, and a white noise machine app does the same on your phone. But the sound you're used to at home, your own fan, your air conditioner, the rain on your roof, isn't on any of them. Here's how to set up a white noise machine that plays your own sounds, and how it compares with the other options.

## A machine, an app, or your own sounds

| | What you get | What to watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A white noise machine** | A box on the nightstand with a few sounds. Some have a real fan inside | One more thing to pack, and only the sounds it came with |
| **A white noise machine app** | Dozens of sounds on the phone you already carry, often with a timer | Someone else's recordings, and some stop when the screen locks |
| **An app that plays your own sounds** | The fan, rain or room you know, recorded or brought in, looped, and mixed with others | Make sure it loops without a click and plays with the screen off |

## Make your own white noise machine on your phone

1. **Pick your sound.** A built-in white, pink or brown noise, or a recording of your own: the fan you sleep with, the air conditioner, steady rain. Not sure which noise? See [white vs pink vs brown noise](/journal/white-vs-pink-vs-brown-noise/).
2. **Record it, if it's yours.** Two or three minutes of something steady, with the phone set down nearby and notifications off. [Your own fan, anywhere](/journal/record-your-fan-noise/) walks through it.
3. **Loop it without a click.** The end has to run into the start smoothly, or you'll hear a bump every time it repeats. Some apps do this for you.
4. **Mix, if you like.** A low noise under your own recording fills in gaps and evens it out.
5. **Set the volume low**, and keep the phone a little away from your head.
6. **Choose how it ends:** all night, or a timer that fades it out.
7. **Keep the phone charging** if it's playing all night.

On an iPhone, Background Sounds in Settings > Accessibility > Audio & Visual is a built-in option with a few noise and nature sounds, and a timer to stop it.

## How to choose

- **Happy with standard sounds?** A machine or most apps will do.
- **Used to one particular sound?** Record it. Nothing else will sound quite like your own fan or room.
- **Travel a lot?** An app on your phone means one less thing to pack.
- **Sharing a room?** Headphones keep the sound to you; a speaker fills the room.
- **Want more than one sound?** Pick an app that plays several at once, each at its own volume.

## Questions people ask

### Can I use my phone as a white noise machine?
Yes. Play a steady sound in an app that keeps going with the screen off, set the volume low, and keep the phone charging if it plays all night. On iPhone, Background Sounds in Settings works without an app.

### How do I make my own white noise?
Record a steady sound you like, such as a fan, an air conditioner or rain, for two or three minutes, then loop it so the end runs into the start. Or use a built-in white, pink or brown noise and mix your recording on top.

### Is there a white noise app that plays my own recordings?
Yes, some white noise apps let you import or record your own sounds. Check that they loop without a click and keep playing with the screen locked. In the Effugium Aura beta you can do both, and mix your sounds with the noise that comes with it.

### Is a white noise machine better than an app?
Neither is better. A machine is one button and doesn't depend on your phone's battery. An app is one less thing to carry, and can play sounds a machine can't, like your own.

### Can I send my white noise to someone else?
With an app that shares sounds, yes. In the newest Effugium Aura test version, a sound you recorded goes through your phone's share sheet as an ordinary audio file anyone can play.

## Bottom line

A white noise machine app turns your phone into a sound machine. For the sound you know best, record your own, loop it without a click, mix it with a little noise if you like, and pick a timer or let it play all night.
