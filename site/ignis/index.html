Ignis Aura

A fantasy tabletop adventure made to be held. Pieces crafted with care, that snap together with a satisfying magnetic click, and a companion app that runs the game, so everyone at the table can stay in the story.

The idea

Part choose-your-own-adventure, part role-playing game, part sharing a story with your friends, in a B-movie fantasy style with dark fantasy themes. Right now it's an early, fully digital proof of concept, and we're building the groundwork and the flow of play. In the end, it's meant to be played with its physical pieces.

Made with care

Every piece is designed to be handled: beautiful to look at, satisfying to snap together, and made to spark the imagination.

Easy to run

The companion app draws the encounters, tells the story and keeps the rules, so nobody has to be the rulebook.

Told together

Choices are made around the table, and the story belongs to everyone who tells it.

How it plays

Keeper of the Flame

One of you is the Keeper of the Flame: they hold the phone, read aloud and keep the game moving. Sharing one phone? The app tells you when to pass it.

No signal needed

Made for a campfire in the woods. Play solo, pass one phone around, or link everyone's phones over Bluetooth, with no internet at all.

Day and night

The world turns from day to night every two rounds, and some things only happen after dark.

A turn

Face what waits on your square, choose what to do, roll the dice, then move on. Roll your own dice, or let the app roll them.

Rune locks

Turn the dials, try the lock, and read the clues it gives back. Open it before your tries run out.

Riddles

Some doors only open to the right answer.

Playing the beta

The physical components are still being designed, so for this early beta the whole experience is playable with just the app. Beta testers play early builds and tell us what to change. At release, the game is played with its physical set, and the app helps run it.
