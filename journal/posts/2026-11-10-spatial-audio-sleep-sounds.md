Most sleep sounds play inside your head: one track, left and right. With spatial audio, each sound can seem to sit in its own place around you: the rain behind you, the fan to your left, a stream ahead. Here's how it works, how to turn it on with AirPods or on a Pixel phone, and how to arrange your sounds the way you want them.

## Stereo, spatial audio, head tracking and 8D

| | What you hear |
| --- | --- |
| **Stereo** | Left and right. With headphones, the sound seems to sit inside your head. |
| **Spatial audio** | Sounds placed around you: in front, behind, to the side, above. |
| **Head tracking** | On headphones that support it, sounds stay put as you turn your head, instead of turning with you. |
| **8D audio** | Usually a stereo track panned round and round your head. |

Spatial audio needs headphones or earbuds. Apple's and Google's own settings work with apps and content that support it, such as films, shows and songs made for it. Apple says it isn't available for all apps or content.

## Turn on spatial audio with AirPods on iPhone

1. **Put in your AirPods** and check they're connected to your iPhone.
2. **Open Control Center**, then touch and hold the volume control.
3. **Tap Spatial Audio**, at the lower right.
4. **Choose Off, Fixed or Head Tracked.** Fixed is spatial audio without head tracking; Head Tracked is spatial audio with it.

Your choice is saved for that app and used again the next time you open it. Apple lists AirPods 3, AirPods 4, AirPods 5, AirPods Pro and AirPods Max for spatial audio and head tracking.

With **Fixed**, sounds are placed around you but turn with your head. With **Head Tracked**, Apple says the audio sounds like it's coming from your iPhone.

### Personalized Spatial Audio

Apple can also make a spatial audio profile for you, from a scan of your face and ears with the TrueDepth camera on an iPhone with iOS 16 or later. With your AirPods connected, open Settings, tap your AirPods' name, then **Audio & Routing** > **Personalized Spatial Audio** > **Personalize Spatial Audio**, and follow the steps on screen.

## Turn on spatial audio on a Pixel phone

1. **Open Settings** and tap **Sound & vibration** > **Spatial audio**.
2. **For wired headphones**, turn on **Wired headphones**. **For wireless ones**, tap **Connected devices settings**, tap your headphones, then turn on **Spatial Audio**.

Head tracking needs Pixel Buds Pro or Pro 2 with a Pixel 6 or newer, and it's off until you turn it on. Open **Settings** > **Connected devices**, tap the settings for your Pixel Buds under Saved devices, then **Device details** > **Sounds**, and turn on **Spatial Audio** and **Head tracking**.

Other Android phones may put these settings somewhere else, or not have them.

## What you can do with sounds around you

- **Give each sound its own place.** Rain and a fan on top of each other blur together. Put them in different places and you can pick each one out.
- **Rebuild a room you know.** The rain at the window, the fan by the bed, the hum of the fridge down the hall: put them back where they are at home.
- **Set each one's volume.** Bring one sound forward, push another into the distance.

## How to arrange your sounds

1. **Start with your main sound** where it is at home: a fan to the left of the bed, an air conditioner above the door.
2. **Add a window** behind you or to one side: rain, wind or the sea.
3. **Add something low** underneath, like brown noise, if you like a fuller sound.
4. **Balance the levels** until nothing stands out more than you want it to.
5. **Keep the volume low**, and raise it a little only if you need to.

Sleeping in earbuds isn't for everyone. If they press on your ears when you lie on your side, try soft sleep earbuds, or play your sounds on a speaker instead. On a speaker, though, the places around you go: the sound comes from the speaker.

## Ways to put sounds around you

| Way | What you do | What to watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A spatial audio track, with your phone's setting on | Play it in an app that supports spatial audio | Someone else chose where each sound sits |
| An 8D video or track | Play it on any headphones | Usually a stereo track circling your head; you can't move the sounds |
| An app that places each sound | Put each sound where you want it and set its volume | Check it keeps playing with the screen off, and try it on your own headphones |

## How to choose

- **Want sounds outside your head, on content made for it?** Turn on Spatial Audio: Fixed or Head Tracked on iPhone, or Spatial audio on a Pixel.
- **Want to choose where every sound sits?** Use an app that places sounds one by one.
- **Listening in bed?** With Head Tracked, Apple says the sound seems to come from your iPhone, so where the phone sits matters. If you'd rather the sounds turn with you when you roll over, try Fixed.
- **Sharing a room?** Headphones keep the sound to you.
- **Only have a speaker?** You'll hear everything from the speaker. Places around you need headphones.

## Questions people ask

### Do I need AirPods for spatial audio sleep sounds?
You need headphones or earbuds, but not always AirPods. Apple's head tracking needs AirPods that support it, and a Pixel's needs Pixel Buds Pro or Pro 2. Apps that place sounds themselves can work on other headphones: in the Effugium Aura beta, your sounds are placed around you on any headphones, so tell us how yours sound.

### How do I turn on spatial audio for AirPods on iPhone?
With your AirPods in, open Control Center, touch and hold the volume control, tap Spatial Audio at the lower right, then choose Fixed or Head Tracked.

### What's the difference between Fixed and Head Tracked?
Both place sounds around you. Fixed has no head tracking, so the sounds turn with your head. Head Tracked follows your head, and Apple says the audio sounds like it's coming from your iPhone.

### What are 8D rain sounds?
Usually a stereo recording of rain panned in circles around your head, made for headphones. With spatial audio, a sound can also sit behind or above you, and with head tracking, stay put as you turn.

### Do spatial sounds work on a speaker?
Not in the same way. Placing a sound behind or above you needs headphones; on a speaker, the sound comes from the speaker.

### Can I put a sound above or below me?
In the Effugium Aura beta, you can: there are places above and below you as well as all around. Height needs headphones. Try it and tell us whether you can hear where it is.

### Will my sounds move when I roll over in bed?
With Fixed, they turn with your head. With Head Tracked on iPhone, Apple says the sound seems to come from your iPhone, so it depends where the phone is. In the Effugium Aura beta, try a night with head tracking on and one with it off, and tell us which you'd keep.

## Bottom line

Spatial audio places sounds around you on headphones, and head tracking keeps them in place as you turn. Turn it on in Control Center on iPhone, or under Sound & vibration on a Pixel. To choose where every sound sits, use an app that places them one by one, keep the volume low, and try it in bed before a whole night.
