Effugium Aura

An audio player for when you need a little peace.

Mix your own sounds, put each one where you want it around you, and let it play through the night with the screen off.

What you can do

Effugium Aura is all about control: your sounds, where they sit, how they move, and when they stop.

Your own sounds

Use sounds you already have, like your fan, the rain on your porch or a creek you recorded, or record one in the app. Effugium helps any sound loop without a gap. A few sounds come with it to get you started, and we may offer sound packs in the future.

All around you

Put each sound in front, behind, to either side, above or below. With headphones, each one sounds like it's where you put it.

Gravity

Let your sounds drift around you on slow orbits, each on its own ring, while the odd shooting star passes by.

The Kicker

A sound, a vibration, or both, on a timer you set. Use it as a bell while you meditate, or as a cue for lucid dream practice.

Fades out on its own

Set a sleep timer and the mix fades out when it ends. It keeps playing with the screen off, with the controls on your lock screen.

Yours

No account and no ads. Your recordings stay on your phone.
