Effugium Aura privacy

Effugium Aura works on iPhone and Android. There's no account to make, and your sounds, recordings and mixes stay on your phone. The app sends us nothing unless you choose to share anonymous counts, or send us a note.

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

What stays on your phone

  • Your sounds, recordings, soundscapes, folders and settings are kept in the app on your phone, and in your phone's own backup if you use one: iCloud on iPhone, your Google backup on Android. We never receive them.
  • The microphone is used only while the field recorder is open, to record. Recordings stay on your phone.
  • Head tracking (iPhone): with headphones that support it, the app uses your headphones' movement on your iPhone, only to keep your sounds in place around you as you turn your head. It isn't kept or sent anywhere.
  • The lock screen panel, StandBy and widgets (iPhone) show your mix from what's on your phone. On Android, the playback controls show it the same way.
  • The Apple Watch app shows and controls your mix by talking to the app on your iPhone directly. Nothing goes through us.
  • The Physical Kicker vibrates your Apple Watch with quiet notifications your iPhone books for itself and passes to your watch. They don't come from us or go through any server, and the app asks to send notifications only if you choose to vibrate your watch. On Android it vibrates the phone itself.

Buying Pro

Pro is bought through Apple's App Store on iPhone, or through Google Play on Android. We don't receive your name, your Apple ID or Google account, or your payment details: the app asks Apple or Google, on your phone, what you own. Apple's or Google's privacy policy covers the purchase.

Anonymous counts, only if you say yes

The app can share a few anonymous numbers to help us make it better. It's off unless you turn it on: the app asks once, and Sharing in its menu turns it on or off and shows exactly what was last sent. While it's on, about once a day it sends:

  • how long you played, and at what time of day;
  • which modes you favor: Flat or Gravity, headphones or speakers;
  • how many sounds are in your mixes, and how many orbit, shooting stars and the Kicker;
  • the sleep timer length you chose, and which lock screen buttons you used;
  • how many recordings you made and saved, and how long they are, and whether you kept or declined a seamless loop;
  • how many nights you used the app this week, a number your phone works out;
  • whether you're on Free or Pro, the app's version, and your phone's model and its iOS or Android version;
  • on iPhone, Apple's crash reports for the app: what went wrong and where in the app's code, with no names in them.

It never sends your sounds or recordings, their names, your mixes' names, or anything you type. There's no account and no ID that links one day's numbers to the next, and we don't store your IP address. The counts are kept by Cloudflare in a private database only Creatio Aura can access, used only to improve the app, and deleted after two years.

To stop, turn Sharing off in the app's menu: nothing more is sent. Because the counts are anonymous, we can't tell which are yours, so we can't find them to delete them sooner.

Notes you send us

Send feedback, in the app's menu, sends what you write (up to 1,000 characters, without links) and, so we can find a problem, the app's version and build and your phone's model. It's anonymous: there's no email field, so we can't reply. It reaches our inbox through our feedback service on Cloudflare, and we delete notes two years after they're sent. Because notes are anonymous, we can't find yours to delete it sooner. If you're offline, the note waits on your phone and goes when it can.

Test versions

If you try a test version through Apple's TestFlight or Google Play's testing, you join with your Apple ID or Google account, and Apple or Google may show us the name or email address you joined with, so we can invite you and send updates. TestFlight also lets you send us screenshots and comments, and Apple may share crash reports from test versions with us. We use these only to run the test.

No ads, no tracking

The app has no ads and no advertising or analytics companies' code, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The app collects nothing that identifies anyone, children included.

Questions

Email privacy@creatio-aura.com. Our website, beta sign-ups and waiting lists are covered by our main privacy policy.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and say so in the app's update notes.
