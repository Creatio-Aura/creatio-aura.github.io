Meditation apps come with their own recordings. But maybe you have your own: a guided session from a teacher, a piece of music you always sit with, or a sound you recorded, like a creek, the rain or the hum of your own room. A meditation audio player for your own recordings plays those, with a timer for the length of your sit. Here's how to set one up on your phone, and which kind of player fits what you want to play.

## Once through, or on repeat?

Meditation audio comes in two kinds, and they want different players.

| What you play | How it plays | What fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A guided recording** | Once, from start to finish | A music or podcast app, or your phone's files app |
| **A piece of music** | Once, or on repeat for a long sit | A music app with repeat, or a player that loops |
| **A steady sound** (a stream, rain, your room) | On repeat for as long as you sit | A player that loops it smoothly, with a timer |
| **A bell every few minutes** | Now and then, above everything else | A meditation timer app, or a player with an interval bell |

## Play your own meditation audio with a timer

1. **Put the recording on your phone.** Save it to your files, or record it yourself: two or three minutes of something steady is plenty for a sound on repeat.
2. **Pick a player that keeps going with the screen locked.** Try a short sit first, with the phone locked, to check.
3. **For a sound on repeat, check the loop.** If the end doesn't run smoothly into the start, you'll hear a bump every time it repeats.
4. **Set a timer for the length of your sit.** On iPhone, the Clock app's timer can stop whatever is playing: in Timers, tap **When Timer Ends**, then **Stop Playing** at the bottom of the list. Some players have a timer of their own that fades the sound out instead of stopping it.
5. **Add a bell, if you like.** A soft sound every few minutes, or a tap on your wrist. [Meditation interval bell](/journal/meditation-interval-bell/) covers how often and which sound.
6. **Turn on Do Not Disturb** and set the volume low before you start, so you don't pick up the phone once you've begun.

## How to choose a meditation audio player

- **Playing a guided recording, once?** A music or podcast app is enough. Pair it with the Clock timer on iPhone, or a sleep timer in the app.
- **Playing a sound on repeat?** Pick a player that loops it without a click and has a timer.
- **Want more than one sound?** Look for one that mixes them, each at its own volume, like a stream under a little rain.
- **Want a bell too?** Check the bell plays above your sounds, and at the interval you choose.
- **Sitting with headphones?** A player that places sounds around you keeps the layers apart.

## Questions people ask

### Can I play my own meditation recordings on my phone?
Yes. Save the recording to your phone and play it in a music, podcast or files app, or in a meditation audio player that plays your own sounds. Check it keeps playing with the screen locked.

### How do I play meditation music with a timer?
Start the music, then set a timer for the length of your sit. On iPhone, set the Clock timer's When Timer Ends to Stop Playing, and the music stops when the timer runs out. Some players have their own timer, and some fade the sound out instead of stopping it.

### Should meditation music loop?
It depends on what you play. A guided recording plays once. A steady sound, like a stream or rain, is easier on repeat, as long as it loops without a click you'd notice every time.

### Can I add a bell to my meditation audio?
Yes, with an app that rings a bell on a timer above other sounds. In the Effugium Aura beta, the Kicker rings a singing bowl, or vibrates your phone or Apple Watch, every few minutes over your mix.

### Can I record my own meditation sounds?
Yes. Set your phone down near a steady sound, like a creek, rain or a fan, and record two or three minutes. [Your own fan, anywhere](/journal/record-your-fan-noise/) walks through it.

## Bottom line

Play a guided recording once, in a music or podcast app with a timer. For a sound you want on repeat, use a meditation audio player that loops it smoothly, then add a timer for your sit and a bell if you like.
