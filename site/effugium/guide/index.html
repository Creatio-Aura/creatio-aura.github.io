Effugium Aura · User guide

Everything Effugium Aura can do

One topic at a time. Pick one from the row at the top, or from the list below.

In short

Choose sounds, put each one in its own place around you, and press Play. Switch to Gravity to set them moving, add shooting stars or a Kicker if you like, set a sleep timer, and lock your phone: it keeps playing.

The main screen

  • The top bar: your soundscape's name, the 3D or Speaker switch, ☰ and Play. Below it, the volume, and Flat or Gravity.
  • The circle: the space around you, with you in the middle.
  • Below it: your sounds in folders, with Record, Import and ⋯ (Folders, Manage), and the Sleep timer.
  • ☰: the menu.

Tap ☰ > Take the tour again any time for the welcome tour.

The basics

Flat

Flat is where you build a mix. The circle is the space around you, with you in the middle, and each sound sits in its own place on it.

In short

Open a folder below the circle, then drag a sound onto a place, or tap the sound and then the place. Press Play.

Place a sound

  1. Below the circle, tap a folder to show its sounds.
  2. Hold a sound and drag it onto a place. Or tap the sound, then tap a place.
  3. Press Play. Each place holds one sound.

The places around you

Twelve places ring the circle: Front, Front right, Right ahead, Right, Right behind, Back right, Behind, Back left, Left behind, Left, Left ahead and Front left. Two more sit Above and Below you, and Low tones is for deep sounds, like a rumble or brown noise.

The names of the empty places show while you're placing a sound; the rest of the time, only your sounds' names do.

Free uses Front, Behind, Left, Right and Low tones. Pro adds the eight in between, Above and Below.

Change a sound

  • Tap a placed sound for its volume and Mute. More holds Loop and Remove.
  • Drag it to another place, or onto Remove.
  • Hold a sound in the list for its options: rename, colour, folders and more (see Sounds).

Headphones or speakers

In the top bar, beside ☰, the switch says 3D for headphones or Speaker: tap it to change. With headphones, each sound seems to come from its own place around you. On the speaker, everything plays in front of you, and Above and Below need headphones to be heard as height.

iPhone With headphones on iPhone, Effugium uses Apple's spatial audio, and AirPods that support it can keep your sounds in place as you turn your head (see Menu and settings).

Effugium always opens in Flat. To set sounds moving, switch to Gravity.

The basics

Gravity

Gravity turns your mix into a sphere around you. Each sound becomes a planet on its own ring, and can circle you.

In short

Build your mix in Flat, then tap Gravity at the top. Tap a planet to choose it, then Orbit to send it round you.

Switch to Gravity

  1. Place your sounds in Flat.
  2. Tap Gravity at the top. Tap Flat to go back.

You add sounds in Flat. In Gravity, To add sounds, switch back to Flat › takes you there.

What you see

You're in the middle. Each sound is a planet on its own ring around you. Faint rings drawn in towards you show the pull; you can change them, or turn them off, under Rings around you.

What lives in Gravity

  • Orbits: sounds circling you, each at its own speed and tilt.
  • Shooting stars: sounds that pass by now and then.
  • The Kicker: a sound or a vibration on a timer.

Shooting stars and the Kicker play while you're in Gravity and the mix is playing. Tap ⓘ beside either for a short explanation.

Free and Pro

In Free, two sounds orbit at a time, at ear level. With Pro, every sound can orbit, on rings tilted over and under you.

Height needs headphones. On speakers, sounds still sweep left and right.

The basics

Orbits and turning

In Gravity, each sound can circle you on its own ring: forwards or backwards, slow or lively, flat or tilted over your head.

In short

In Gravity, tap a planet, then Orbit. Reverse turns it the other way, and Speed sets how fast.

Set a sound orbiting

  1. In Gravity, tap a planet to choose it.
  2. Tap Orbit to send it round you.
  3. Reverse turns it the other way: forwards or backwards.
  4. Speed: Slow, Calm or Lively.

Tilt its ring Pro

The Ring slider tilts a ring from Flat, at ear level, to Upright, passing over your head and under you.

Move a sound on its ring

Drag a planet up or down its ring to move it.

Turn the view

Drag sideways on the sky to turn the view round.

Random and Reset

  • Random sends every sound orbiting on its own random tilt, direction and speed.
  • Reset puts every sound back in its own place and ring. It asks first.

In Free, two sounds orbit at a time, at ear level. Pro lets every sound orbit, at any tilt.

  • Rename and Colour
  • Remove from library: it's taken out of your library and every soundscape, with any loop you made for it. The file you imported from stays where it was.

Your sounds

Field recorder

Record a sound right in the app: the rain on your porch, a creek on a walk, the fan you're used to. Then trim it, make it loop and place it in your mix.

In short

Tap Record below the circle, or ☰ > Field recorder. Tap the red button to record, then Stop and review.

Before you record

  • Nothing is recorded until you tap the red button.
  • Hold the phone still, pointed at the sound, or set it down near it.
  • Wind cut takes out the low rumble of wind and of the phone moving in your hand (below 80 hertz). Set it before you record.

While you record

  • The meter and the dotted peak line show how loud it gets. It tells you if it's a good level, very quiet (move closer if you can) or too loud (move back a little).
  • Tap Mark at any moment you might want to cut at later.
  • Pause stops recording until you carry on.
  • It keeps recording with the screen locked: up to a minute at a time in Free, up to ten minutes with Pro.
  • Record at least a couple of seconds to keep it, and 4 seconds or more to make it loop.

After you stop

  • The whole recording stays on the tape. Tap ▶ to hear it.
  • Drag the two peach handles to leave out the start or the end. It zooms in so you can fine-tune.
  • Fades softens the start and the end. Give it a Name.
  • Make it seamless Pro: the tape wraps into a ring, starting at the top like a clock, and the loop helper finds where the end can flow back into the start. Tap the middle of the ring to hear it going round. Longest repeats least often, Smoothest has the join that's hardest to hear. Hear the join plays the moment it starts again, Blend sets how gently it fades across, and Undo unwraps it.
  • Split at the marks saves each part between your marks as a sound of its own.

Keeping it

When you save, it asks which folders the recording goes in. Then it joins your sounds: tap Place it in the mix, or Record another. Discarding asks first.

Storage

It records in mono at 48 kHz, without losing detail. While you record, the take uses about 9 MB a minute until you save or discard it. Your recordings stay on your phone, and nothing is uploaded. ⋯ then Manage shows how much room each one takes and lets you remove them.

Tap ⓘ in the recorder for How the recorder works.

Your sounds

Loop Studio

Loop Studio is Effugium's loop helper. It makes a recording play on and on, with no bump where it starts again. Pro

In short

Open ☰ > Loop Studio and pick a sound, or tap Loop on a placed sound. Choose where it joins, hear Before and After, then tap Use this loop.

What it does

  • It trims any silence from the start and the end.
  • It listens through the whole sound for two moments that sound almost the same, and joins the end back to the start there, blending one into the other.
  • It checks what would happen if the recording simply repeated: a click, a gap of silence or a jump in loudness.
  • It notices sudden sounds, like a bark or a car door, and tells you if one would repeat every time round.

Open it

  • ☰ menu > Loop Studio, then pick a sound.
  • Tap a placed sound, then More and Loop.
  • Hold a sound in the list, then Fine-tune in Loop Studio.
  • After you import, Make seamless makes the loop for you; fine-tune it here any time.

Where should it join?

  • Smoothest join: the hardest to hear where it starts again.
  • Longer and Longest: repeat less often. Shorter ones repeat more often.
  • Without the sudden sounds: leaves out a bark or a bang so it won't come round again.
  • The whole sound: everything, from start to end.

Hear the difference

Play Before, then After: Before is the recording just repeating, After is your loop.

Blend at the join

How long the end and the start overlap. Longer is softer; shorter keeps it crisp. Then pick how they blend:

  • Steady level keeps the loudness even through the join. Best for rain, wind, fans and other rushing sounds.
  • Exact match lines the sound up exactly at the join, so even a short blend is enough. Best for steady hums and tones.

Endless

Plays random parts of the sound, each blending into the next, so it never repeats the same way. Good for short sounds.

Finish

Use this loop · Next sound keeps it and moves on. You can skip a sound for now, or go Back to the automatic loop. Tap ⓘ for How the loop helper works.

Extras

Shooting stars

A shooting star is a sound that passes by now and then in a straight line, like a far-off train in the night. It plays once, then waits for its next pass. Pro

In short

In Gravity, under Shooting stars, tap Add. Pick a sound, which way it passes and how often.

Add a shooting star

  1. Switch to Gravity.
  2. Under Shooting stars, tap Add.
  3. Sound: Raven passing, Ship horn or Wave on the rocks, or All sounds › for any other.
  4. Which way: Side to side, Front to back or Up and down, and Turn it round sends it the other way. Front to back passes by on your right; up and down passes in front of you.
  5. How often: a pause of 10, 20 or 30 seconds or a minute after each pass, or Now and then for a random pause of 10 seconds to 2 minutes.
  6. Set its Volume. Tap the sound to hear one pass now (it starts the mix if it's paused).
  7. Tap Add shooting star.

The first pass comes a couple of seconds after you add it, while the mix is playing. You can have up to six. Tap one to change it, or Remove this shooting star.

Up and down needs headphones.

Extras

Kicker

The Kicker is a cue on a timer, above everything else: a sound, a vibration, or both. Use it as a bell to come back to while you meditate, or as a cue for lucid dream practice. Pro

In short

In Gravity, under Kicker, tap Add. Choose what it does and how often, then tap Add the Kicker.

Add the Kicker

  1. Switch to Gravity.
  2. Under Kicker, tap Add.
  3. Kick with: Sound, Vibrate or Both. Vibrate is a silent kick: only you feel it, and nothing plays, so nobody beside you is woken.
  4. How often: every minute up to every two hours. A minute less and A minute more change it.
  5. Tap Add the Kicker.

The first one comes one interval after you add it. The timer waits while the mix is paused, and the Kicker shows when the next one is due.

The sound

  • The sounds made for it come first: Singing bowl, Big singing bowl, Temple bell and Wind chimes. All sounds › shows the rest.
  • Timing and volume, folded until you tap it, shows what's set, and holds:
  • Which part plays: move where it starts and stops.
  • Volume: its own level, above the mix. The sleep fade doesn't change it; the phone's volume does.
  • Lower the other sounds while it plays: the mix dips gently, then comes back as the sound fades.

Vibrations

Pick Vibrate or Both and a Vibration row opens with these, folding away again when you tap it:

  • Vibrate on: Phone, Watch or Both. Watch shows when your iPhone has an Apple Watch paired. On Android, it vibrates your phone.
  • When it vibrates: With the sound, or Before as a heads-up: a vibration, then the sound.
  • Vibrations each time: one, two or three, a few seconds apart.
  • Try it now (or Hear it now), above Add, plays or vibrates it once, as set.

On your Apple Watch iPhone

  • Your watch vibrates while your iPhone is locked. If the phone is in your hand, the phone shows the kick instead.
  • Each vibration arrives as a quiet notification named Kick 1, Kick 2, Kick 3. Tap Allow notifications when asked.
  • If you use Sleep Focus, let Effugium through: in Settings, Focus, Sleep, add Effugium under Apps. Otherwise Sleep Focus holds the vibrations back until morning.
  • If your watch chimes as well, turn on its Silent Mode: press the side button, then tap the bell.
  • Send a test vibration in 10 s, then lock your phone, to check it.

Extras

Binaural beats

A binaural beat is two plain tones, one in each ear, a few hertz apart. You hear the difference between them as a slow beat. Effugium plays them; it makes no claims about what they do.

In short

Open the Binaural folder and drop a beat on Left or Right: its pair fills the other side. Use headphones.

The five beats

From the slowest to the fastest: Delta (3 beats a second), Theta (6), Alpha (10), Beta (16) and Gamma (40). Free has Alpha; Pro has all five, and journeys.

Add a beat

  1. Open the Binaural folder below the circle.
  2. Drop a beat on Left or Right, or tap it, then Left or Right. Its pair fills the other side.
  3. Tap it for its Volume, or Remove the pair.

A beat stays in place: it doesn't move, turn or orbit.

Headphones

Binaural beats need headphones. On speakers, the same beat plays as a soft pulse from both sides.

Journeys Pro

A journey rides from one beat to the next, gliding gently between them.

  1. Drop Journey from the Binaural folder on Left or Right.
  2. Set the layers, in order: which beat, and for how many minutes. + Add a layer adds another.
  3. Choose whether to Glide between layers, then Start the journey.

It starts from the top each time, and waits while the mix is paused. You can change the journey or end it from the beat's panel.

At night

Sleep timer

Play your mix all night, or set a sleep timer that fades it slowly to silence. It keeps working with the screen off.

In short

Below your sounds, tap Sleep timer, then pick 15 or 30 minutes, an hour or two hours.

Set it

  1. Below your sounds, tap Sleep timer. The row says what's set; tapping it shows the choices.
  2. Pick 15 min, 30 min, 1 hour or 2 hours, or turn it off.

It shows the time it will be silent by: Fades out until silent at about…

How the fade works

The fade takes up the last part of the timer: a third of it, up to ten minutes. A 15-minute timer fades over its last 5 minutes; 30 minutes or more fade over the last 10. Then the mix stops.

The Kicker keeps its own level through the fade.

Fade out now

iPhone On the lock screen, Fade out now fades the mix over a minute, then stops it. Cancel fade out changes your mind (see Lock screen).

Lock screen

Effugium keeps playing with the screen off, and you can pause it, change the volume or fade it out without unlocking your phone.

In short

While a mix plays, its controls show on your lock screen. On iPhone, they show in StandBy too.

On iPhone iPhone

While a mix plays, a panel shows on your lock screen, with a picture of your mix, when it fades, and:

  • Pause and Play
  • Quieter and Louder: one step of the side buttons each
  • Fade out now, which turns into Cancel fade out while it fades

StandBy: with your iPhone charging on its side, the same panel shows large.

Widgets: Last played starts your last mix, and Favourites shows the soundscapes you've starred (see Soundscapes). Add them from your home screen or StandBy.

If Live Activities are turned off for Effugium, the app tells you, with a button to open Settings.

On Android

The first time you press Play, Android asks whether Effugium can send notifications. Choose Allow: that's how its playback controls show on your lock screen and when you swipe down from the top of the screen.

Volume

The volume slider in the app is your phone's own volume. The side buttons move it, and so do Quieter and Louder on the lock screen, and the crown on your Apple Watch.

Menu and settings

☰ opens the menu: your tools, how Effugium looks and sounds, sharing and feedback.

In short

Tap ☰ on the main screen.

Your tools

  • Loop Studio: make a sound loop without a bump.
  • Field recorder: record a sound to loop and place.
  • Your sounds: import, rename, make seamless.
  • Soundscapes: open, rename or start a new one.

Rings around you

How the rings look in Gravity: Lying flat (faint rings lie flat around you and are drawn in), Dotted (rings of dots crowd together as they're drawn in) or Off (a still pool: just you and your sounds).

Head tracking iPhone

With AirPods that support it. On: sounds stay put when you turn your head. Off: sounds turn with your head. It's in Free and Pro.

Sharing

Off unless you turn it on. When it's on, Effugium sends anonymous counts about once a day, and only these: how long you played and at what time of day, Flat or Gravity and headphones or speakers, how many sounds are in your mixes, the sleep timer and the lock screen buttons, recordings you've made, how many nights you used it this week, Free or Pro, and the app's version.

Never your sounds or recordings, their names, or your mixes' names. No account, no ID that links one day to the next, no ads, nothing sold. Show the last one sent shows exactly what went.

Send feedback

A note to the people who make the app. It goes with the app's version and your phone's model, and nothing else, so add your name if you'd like a reply.
