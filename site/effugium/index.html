Effugium Aura

An audio player for when you need a little peace.

Mix your own sounds, put each one where you want it around you, and let it play through the night with the screen off.

What you can do

Effugium Aura is all about control: your sounds, where they sit, how they move, and when they stop.

Your own sounds

Use sounds you already have, like your fan, the rain on your porch or a creek you recorded, or record one in the app. Effugium helps any sound loop more seamlessly. A few sounds come with it to get you started, and we may offer sound packs in the future.

All around you

Put each sound in front, behind, to either side, above or below. With headphones, each one sounds like it's where you put it.

Gravity

Let your sounds drift around you on slow orbits, each on its own ring, while the odd shooting star passes by.

The Kicker

A sound, a vibration, or both, on a timer you set. Use it as a bell while you meditate, or as a cue for lucid dream practice.

Fades out on its own

Set a sleep timer and the mix fades out when it ends. It keeps playing with the screen off, with the controls on your lock screen.

Yours

No account and no ads. Your recordings stay on your phone.

A field recorder in your pocket

Record a sound right where it is: the rain on your porch, a creek on a walk, the fan you're used to. Then trim it, make it loop and place it in your mix.

Choose your mic

Use one of your phone's own mics, a USB-C mic, a wireless mic's receiver or a Bluetooth mic. On iPhones that can, All around records with every mic at once, for a sound that surrounds you.

A good level, less wind

A meter shows how loud it gets and tells you if it's too quiet or too loud. Wind cut takes out the deepest rumble of wind and of the phone moving in your hand.

Keeps going when you lock it

It keeps recording with the screen locked. Pause when you need to, and mark moments to cut at later.

Stays on your phone

Your recordings stay on your phone, and nothing is uploaded. Share one with someone if you like.

Seamless loops

A sound that repeats all night should flow back into itself. Effugium helps find where each sound's end can join its start more seamlessly.

Make seamless

When you record or import a sound, Effugium offers to make it loop more seamlessly. Your originals are never changed.

Loop Studio

The sound wraps into a ring. Pick Longest, which repeats least often, or Smoothest, the join that's hardest to hear. Hear the join, set how gently it blends, and undo any time.

Trim, fade and split

Leave out the start or the end, soften both with a fade, or split a recording at your marks into sounds of their own.

Watch your mix

Visualize shows your sounds full screen, a dozen ways: visualizers that follow each sound, where it is, how loud and its colour, and a campfire to watch while they play.

Sideways, full screen

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger picture. The rest of the app stays upright.

Fades to black

After 15 minutes, 30 minutes or an hour, the picture fades and stops drawing, and your sounds keep playing.

Look around

On iPhone, with AirPods that follow your head, turn your head to look around the night sky and the scenes.

Your colours, your pace

Colours from your mix or new ones, and Gentler motion. A short note about light sensitivity comes first.
