If you're used to the sound of a fan, an air conditioner or the rain on your own window at night, a hotel room can sound all wrong. You can take that exact sound with you: record it once at home, loop it so the join doesn't click, and play it wherever you stay, as loud as you like.

## Your fan, not a fan sound

Every fan sounds a little different: the pitch of the motor, the hum of the blades, a faint rattle you stopped hearing years ago. Apps and videos are full of fan sounds, but they aren't yours. If the one you're used to is in your own bedroom, that's the one to record.

A recording also packs smaller than a fan. It weighs nothing, needs no plug, and plays on a plane, in a tent or on a friend's sofa.

## How to record your fan

Ten minutes of care at home makes the difference between a loop you stop noticing and one that gives itself away.

1. **Set the fan the way it is at night.** The same speed, the same spot, the window open or shut as usual.
2. **Turn on Do Not Disturb**, so a buzz or a ping doesn't end up in the recording and come back every few minutes. On iPhone, open Control Center, touch and hold the button that shows Focus or Do Not Disturb, then tap Do Not Disturb. On Android, swipe down from the top of the screen and tap Do Not Disturb (on some phones it's under Modes).
3. **Set the phone down about an arm's length from the fan**, on a shelf or a chair, not touching it. Holding it adds rustles; resting it on the fan adds rattles.
4. **Wait for quiet.** Voices, a dog, a car door: anything that happens once stands out when it repeats.
5. **Record two or three minutes.** That's plenty for a steady fan, and a longer recording repeats less often.

The same steps work for an air conditioner, a humidifier, a fish tank or rain on the window. Rain changes more than a fan, so give it a little longer.

## How to loop it without a click

A recording played on repeat has a seam where the end meets the start. If the sound jumps there, you hear a click or a bump every couple of minutes. To hide the seam:

1. **Trim the ends.** The first and last seconds often hold the tap of your finger or a change in the room.
2. **Join it where the sound is steady**, not in the middle of a rattle or a gust.
3. **Blend the end into the start** over a second or two, so the loudness doesn't jump.

Then play it in an app that repeats one sound and keeps going with the screen off.

## Ways to play it on repeat

| Way | What you do | What to watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An audio player with repeat | Open the recording and set it to repeat one track | Nothing smooths the join, so a click can come round each time |
| An audio editor, then a player | Trim and blend the ends in the editor, save a copy, play that on repeat | More steps, and you redo them for each recording |
| An app for your own sleep sounds | Record or bring in the sound, let the app smooth the join, play it all night | Check that it plays with the screen off and ends the way you want |

## How to choose

- **Just for one trip?** Any player with repeat works if your recording joins cleanly. Listen to the join before you leave.
- **Can you hear a click?** Use an editor, or an app that blends the join for you.
- **Want other sounds too**, like rain behind the fan? Look for an app that plays several at once, each at its own volume.
- **Sharing the room?** Use headphones or earbuds so it plays only for you. On a small speaker, keep it low and away from your head.
- **Decide how the night ends:** all night, or a sleep timer that fades it out.

## Questions people ask

### Can I loop a voice memo on iPhone?
Save the memo from Voice Memos to the Files app, then open it in an app that plays audio on repeat. Or skip that step and record straight into an app that loops.

### How long should a fan recording be?
Two or three minutes is plenty for a steady fan, as long as the join is smooth. Record rain a little longer, since it changes more.

### Why does my loop click every time it repeats?
The end of the recording doesn't line up with the start. Trim the ends, and blend the end into the start over a second or two.

### Will it keep playing with the screen locked?
That depends on the app, so check before your trip. Effugium Aura plays with the screen off, all night or until its sleep timer fades it out, with its controls on the lock screen.

### Can I record an air conditioner or rain the same way?
Yes. Anything steady works the same way: an air conditioner, a humidifier, a fish tank, rain on the window.

### Can I play it without bothering someone in the room?
Play it through headphones or earbuds, so only you hear it.
