Some people like their white noise all night. Others only want it while they fall asleep, then quiet. Either way it's your choice, and so is how it ends: a sudden stop, or a slow fade to silence. Here's how to make sleep sounds stop after you fall asleep on iPhone and Android, and how to get a fade instead of a stop.

## All night, a stop, or a fade?

| | What happens | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **All night** | The sound plays until you stop it | Keep the phone charging, and the volume low |
| **A timer that stops** | The sound plays, then stops at once | Built into iPhone; on Android, it's up to the app |
| **A timer that fades** | The sound gets quieter over several minutes, then stops | Needs an app with a fading sleep timer |

## Stop sleep sounds on a timer on iPhone

1. **Open the Clock app** and tap **Timers**.
2. **Tap When Timer Ends**, then tap **Stop Playing** at the bottom of the list of sounds.
3. **Set how long** and start the timer. When it runs out, whatever is playing stops: music, a podcast or a sleep sounds app.

**Using Background Sounds**, Apple's built-in sleep sounds? In iOS 26 and later it has its own timer: in Settings, go to **Accessibility** > **Audio & Visual** > **Background Sounds**, then tap **Stop Sounds with a Timer**.

Both end the same way: the sound is there, and then it isn't.

## Stop sleep sounds on a timer on Android

On Android, the simplest way is a sleep timer in the app that's playing:

1. **Look for a sleep timer in the app you're using.** Many music, podcast and sleep sounds apps have one, often in the player's menu.
2. **Set how long**, then lock your phone.
3. **Check it the first night**: start a short timer with the screen locked, and make sure the sound stops.

## A stop or a fade

A fade lowers the sound over several minutes until it's silent, so there's no sudden change in the room. If you'd rather the quiet came little by little, a fading timer is the way to go.

- **Pick a timer a little longer than it usually takes you to fall asleep**, so the fade starts after you're out.
- **Keep the volume low** to begin with; a fade from a low level is gentler.
- **If you wake in the night**, start it again for a shorter time.

## How to choose

- **Happy with a sudden stop?** The iPhone Clock timer's Stop Playing works with almost any app. On Android, use the app's own sleep timer.
- **Want a slow fade?** Pick a sleep sounds app whose timer fades the sound out.
- **Want it all night?** Leave the timer off, and keep the phone charging.
- **Using Background Sounds on iPhone?** Its own timer is in Settings, in iOS 26 and later.

## Questions people ask

### How do I make white noise stop after I fall asleep on iPhone?
Open the Clock app, tap Timers, tap When Timer Ends and choose Stop Playing, then start the timer. It stops whatever is playing when it runs out. Background Sounds has its own timer in Settings, in iOS 26 and later.

### Does the Clock app's Stop Playing work with any app?
It stops whatever is playing on your iPhone when the timer runs out, such as music, podcasts and sleep sounds apps.

### How do I stop white noise on a timer on Android?
Use the sleep timer in the app that's playing. Many music, podcast and sleep sounds apps have one, often in the player's menu.

### Can sleep sounds fade out instead of stopping?
Yes, with an app whose sleep timer fades the sound out. In the Effugium Aura beta, the mix fades over the last part of the timer, up to ten minutes, then stops.

### Can I keep sleep sounds playing all night?
Yes. Leave the timer off, keep the volume low, and keep your phone charging.

## Bottom line

On iPhone, the Clock timer's Stop Playing ends any app's sound when it runs out; on Android, use the app's own sleep timer. For a slow fade to silence instead of a sudden stop, use a sleep sounds app whose timer fades out, or leave it playing all night.
