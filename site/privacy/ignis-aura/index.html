Ignis Aura privacy

Ignis Aura is in beta, on iPhone, Android and the web. There's no account to make, and your heroes and games stay on your phone. The app sends us nothing about you, except crash reports with no names in them, the messages of an online game while it's on, and notes you choose to send us.

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

What stays on your phone

  • Your heroes, games, players' names and settings are kept in the app on your phone, and in your phone's own backup if you use one. We never receive them.
  • Bluetooth is used only to join the phones around your table into one game. The phones talk to each other directly; nothing goes through us, and Bluetooth is never used to find your location.
  • The motion sensor is used only while the game asks you to tilt your phone. It isn't kept or sent anywhere.

Playing online

Phones in an online game can't reach each other directly, so they connect through our relay on Cloudflare, which passes the game's messages along: the moves, the heroes and the names you play with, and the group chat. The relay doesn't read or keep them, except for up to 100 messages it holds while the host's phone is away, so they arrive when it's back. A game's room, and everything in it, is deleted once nobody has connected to it for a day. We don't store IP addresses.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry, the service we use to find and fix problems: what went wrong and where in the app's code, the app's version, and your phone's model and its system version. It's set to send nothing about you: no names, no IP address and nothing from your game. Sentry keeps reports for up to 90 days.

Notes you send us

Send feedback, in the app's menu, sends what you write, the email address you give if you'd like a reply (it's optional), and what helps us find a problem: the app's version, the kind of device, the screen you were on, and how far into the game you were (the kind of game, how many heroes, the round). The app adds no names to it. It comes to our inbox through our feedback service on Cloudflare. We keep it until the beta ends, or sooner if you ask us to delete it. If you're offline, the note waits on your phone and goes when it can.

Updates

The app checks Expo, the service we deliver updates through, for a newer version of the game, and downloads it. The check tells Expo the app's version, whether it's an iPhone or Android, and a random number for this copy of the app that Expo uses to roll updates out gradually. None of it says who you are.

The web version

The web version is for beta testers. You sign in with the email you signed up with, and we email you a link; your browser then keeps a sign-in cookie for 30 days, and nothing else. Your games are kept in your browser. Our main privacy policy covers the beta list and that email.

Test versions

If you try a test version through Apple's TestFlight, you join with your Apple ID, and Apple may show us the name or email address you joined with, so we can invite you and send updates. TestFlight also lets you send us screenshots and comments, and Apple may share crash reports from test versions with us. We use these only to run the test.

No ads, no tracking

The app has no ads and no advertising or analytics companies' code, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The beta is for people 13 and over. The app collects nothing that identifies anyone, unless you type it into a note to us.

Questions

Email privacy@creatio-aura.com. Our website, beta sign-ups and waiting lists are covered by our main privacy policy.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and say so in the app's What's new.
