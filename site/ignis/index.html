Ignis Aura

A fantasy tabletop adventure made to be held. Pieces crafted with care, that snap together with a satisfying magnetic click, and a companion app that runs the game, so everyone at the table can stay in the story.

Beta sign-ups openiPhone · Android · Web

The idea

Part choose-your-own-adventure, part a night of telling a story together. Ignis Aura is theater of the mind, anchored by pieces you can pick up and a table of friends around them.

Made with care

Every piece is designed to be handled: beautiful to look at, satisfying to snap together, and made to spark the imagination.

Easy to run

The companion app draws the encounters, tells the story and keeps the rules, so nobody has to be the rulebook.

Told together

Choices are made around the table, and the story belongs to everyone who tells it.

Playing the beta

Beta testers play early builds and tell us what to change. At release, the game is played with its physical set, and the app helps run it.
